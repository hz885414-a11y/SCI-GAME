# Modern Town Tileset - Free Sample (Chucky Assets)

19 top-down 16x16 pixel-art tiles and props from the Modern Town series: an exam bed, a blood-pressure monitor (animated),
an eye chart, waiting chairs and a bench, an exit sign (animated) and two floors from the hospital set; a canned-food shelf,
a soda endcap, a cake dome, a donut case, a checker floor and an "open 24h" neon sign from the supermarket set; a round
clock, a map stand, a mind-the-gap sign, a white tile floor and a platform edge from the subway set.

Every item comes **with and without shadow**, in **16, 32 and 48 px** (`sprites/<shadow|noshadow>/<size>/`).
Animated items also have an `_anim*f` strip (frames side by side). Works in Godot, Unity, GameMaker, Tiled and RPG Maker.

Full packs (same style, they mix in one map):
- Modern Hospital & Clinic: https://chuckyassets.itch.io/modern-hospital-clinic-tileset
- Modern Supermarket & Mini-Mart: https://chuckyassets.itch.io/modern-supermarket-tileset
- Modern Subway & Train Station: https://chuckyassets.itch.io/subway-station-tileset
